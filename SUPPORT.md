# MapEverything Support

For bug reports, questions, and feature requests, open an issue at
https://github.com/salsicha/MapEverything/issues.

You may also email salsicha@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, include the app version and build number, device
model, iOS version, whether ROS or Save Local was enabled, and the relevant
diagnostic or rosbag metadata. Do not include API keys, credentials, or private
sensor recordings in a public issue.
